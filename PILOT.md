# SentinelLayer Pilot Program (Blueprint 29,30)

## Target (1.8 + 29)
- 3 qualified pilots (100-1000 employees, API-heavy, cloud-native)
- ICP validation: 20+ interviews minimum
- Success metrics:
  - WAF false positive rate < 0.5%
  - Risk detection accuracy > 92%
  - Average response time < 20ms
  - Customer satisfaction (NPS) > 80
  - Pilot conversion rate > 60%

## Legal & Regulatory (30)
- UU PDP + OWASP Top 10
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 (cardholder data)
- SOC 2 + ISO 27001 + NIST CSF
- EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting prep (11 Sep 2026)
- Evidence: Full ROPA, DPIA, SLA, DPA, audit trail

## Pilot Workflow
- Onboarding: 7 days
- Monitoring: 30 days
- Review: Weekly with External Retainer
- Handoff: Commercial GA if success
